

# Royal Weave — Premium Men's Fabric Collection Website

## Brand Name: **Royal Weave**
*Tagline: "Craft Your Style with Premium Fabrics"*

## Design System
- **Colors**: Black (#0A0A0A), Gold (#D4AF37), White (#FFFFFF), Dark Navy (#1B2A4A)
- **Typography**: Elegant serif for headings (Playfair Display), clean sans-serif for body (Inter)
- **Style**: Premium, masculine, luxurious with subtle gold accents and smooth animations

## Single-Page Layout (Smooth Scrolling)

### 1. Navigation Bar
- Sticky top nav with brand logo/name "Royal Weave"
- Links: Home, About, Collections, Why Choose Us, Contact
- Mobile hamburger menu
- Gold accent on active/hover states

### 2. Hero Section
- Full-width hero with gradient overlay on fabric imagery
- Headline: "Craft Your Style with Premium Fabrics"
- Two CTAs: "Explore Collection" and "Visit Store"
- Subtle fade-in animations

### 3. About Us Section
- Split layout: text + decorative element
- Brief story about quality fabrics, latest trends, customer satisfaction

### 4. Collections Section
- 4 category cards with hover effects:
  - Formal Fabrics
  - Casual Fabrics
  - Wedding & Ethnic Collection
  - Premium Designer Fabrics
- Each card with image, title, short description
- Image gallery slider for featured fabrics

### 5. Why Choose Us
- 4 feature cards with icons:
  - Premium Quality Fabrics
  - Affordable Pricing
  - Latest Designs
  - Trusted by Customers

### 6. Contact Section
- Three-column layout: Info, Map, Form
- Address, phone numbers with clickable call buttons
- Embedded Google Map (iframe)
- Contact form: Name, Phone, Message

### 7. Footer
- Quick links, social media (Instagram, WhatsApp)
- Copyright text

### 8. Floating WhatsApp Button
- Fixed bottom-right WhatsApp chat button linked to provided number

## Technical Notes
- Fully responsive (mobile-first)
- Smooth scroll between sections
- Fade/scale animations on scroll
- SEO-friendly meta tags
- Uses Unsplash placeholder images for fabrics
